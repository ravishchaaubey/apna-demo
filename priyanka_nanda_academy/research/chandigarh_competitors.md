Even the branded query "Priyanka Nanda Makeup Academy Chandigarh" did not surface the site in a standard search and needed an extended search. [FACT, indicative: US-based tool] (S-LC-51)
4. **Name ambiguity is real.** "Priyanka Makeup Artist" (WeddingWire, Sector 7, 4.1/5 from 11 reviews), Priyanka Sethi, Priyanka Trehan, "Hair Makeup By Priyanka" (Kharar) and a Pune "Nanda Makeup Artist" are different people. None can be attributed to PNA. (S-LC-03, S-LC-06, S-LC-07)
5. **The tricity makeup-education market is crowded and clustered in Sector 34A.** Orane, Lakmé, VLCC, Zuri and Cassia are all in Sector 34A. Cleopatra is in Sector 9-D, Fashion Make Fashion at SCO 24, and PNA's studio is a second-floor shop in the Sector 8 Inner Market. Mohali and Zirakpur have Headmasters, Knuckout, Nav Brar and Parthi Chopra's The Academy. We found no dedicated Panchkula academy. (S-LC-08 to S-LC-25, S-LC-38)
6. **Fee transparency is the exception, not the norm.** Of roughly 15 institutions reviewed, only Zuri publishes a fee ladder online (and the page looks half-finished). Nav Brar publishes one short-course price (INR 15,000, 2022 PDF). Chains and most independents gate fees behind an enquiry. (S-LC-13, S-LC-28)
7. **Indicative local fee bands [EST]:** short skill/self-makeup courses INR 6,500-25,000; 1-2 month certificate from about INR 25,000; 3-6 month professional diploma INR 35,000-75,000; 9-month advanced/"celebrity" tracks INR 70,000-1,20,000; chain flagship advanced courses about INR 1.3-1.5 lakh (Lakmé, pan-India press release). Government-aided diplomas sit at INR 3,730 to 35,000 for 12 months. Evidence is thin and mostly unverified.
8. **The closest peers to PNA are founder-led:** Nav Brar (Mohali/Chandigarh, small-batch one-month pro course, sponsored press, WhatsApp-led) and Tanvi KG (celebrity/film MUA, two-week certified course, Google 4.9 from 125 reviews displayed on her own site). Both lead with credibility assets PNA does not yet show publicly. (S-LC-27 to S-LC-34)
9. **White space [HYP]:** (a) fee- and syllabus-transparent bridal/professional course ladder, (b) a visible, verifiable credential story, (c) WhatsApp-first counselling with a demo/trial class, (d) Panchkula/Zirakpur/Mohali coverage, (e) Sector 8 boutique studio-plus-academy positioning against the Sector 34A institute cluster.
10. **Major gaps:** no PNA fees/durations/certifications, no verified Google ratings for anyone, no Amritsar data, no PMKVY/government centre list for the tricity, and four target search queries (bridal makeup course Chandigarh, professional makeup course Chandigarh, hair and makeup academy Chandigarh, makeup artist course Mohali) were not run because the shared web-search budget ran out; Mohali, Panchkula and Zirakpur were run as close variants only. ## 4. Short competitor profiles

**Orane Signature Academy (chain, Sector 34A).** Scale player in the same lane PNA would target. Strong conversion machinery: enquiry form, free counselling, 0% EMI, named finance partners, "100% job assistance" and employer names (Nykaa, Kaya, Enrich, Lakmé). Weak on transparency: no fee published offline and its own pages disagree on makeup course length (3 weeks/120 h vs 4 months/218 h). Implication for PNA: do not compete on credentials or breadth. Compete on specificity (bridal), small batches, and published prices. [CLAIM for all Orane statements; S-LC-08, S-LC-10]

**Lakmé Academy (chain, Sectors 17E and 34A).** Brand-led, with hour-based course architecture (92 h foundation, 152 h advanced, 260 h international pathway) and a pan-India premium anchor of INR 1.3-1.5 lakh for a 176-hour media/fashion course. Candid about not guaranteeing placement. Implication: sets the ceiling PNA is judged against; PNA's price/value story needs to be explicit relative to this anchor. [S-LC-19, S-LC-20, S-LC-21]

**VLCC Institute (chain, Sector 34-A).** Cosmetology and nutrition heavy; makeup is a short certificate. Offers a free demo class, a cheap trial mechanism PNA can copy. Data on the Chandigarh centre is dated (2018 listing). [S-LC-23, S-LC-24]

**Zuri International Beauty Academy (independent, Sector 34A).** The only competitor with a published fee ladder, EMI figures per month, a "Free Demo Class" button and a keyword-rich "makeup course in Chandigarh with fees" page. Claims 5,000+ students since 2015 and CIBTAC/CIDESCO/IAO recognition (graduates "can obtain"). Two cautions: the page shows draft artefacts, and the CIBTAC page implies a centre listing rather than proof of accreditation. Implication: Zuri is the price-visibility benchmark. If PNA publishes fees, Zuri's bands are the reference point. [CLAIM; S-LC-12, S-LC-13]

**Cassia Makeup Academy (independent, Sector 34A).** Clean, level-based course ladder with durations from 15 days to 5 months and short airbrush/HD add-ons, plus "Easy EMI". No fees. Implication: a ladder with short add-on modules is the common structure; PNA can adopt it and add price transparency. [S-LC-15]

**Fashion Make Fashion (independent, SCO 24).** Heavy SEO and form capture (syllabus download, scholarship, refund, franchise). Credential claims include an implausible "Estd. by Planning Commission" line; this is a reminder that credential claims are noisy in this market and buyers may discount them. [S-LC-16]

**Cleopatra Academy (founder-led, Sector 9-D).** Long-running (own site says since 2002; a ~2014 trade profile says 2009), strong skill-council claims (B&WSSC partner, PMKVY). Website quality is weak (placeholder "$" pricing, no WhatsApp). Implication: skill-council affiliation is a credibility lever; PNA could pursue a B&WSSC/NSDC-aligned assessment tie-up if it fits cost. [HYP; S-LC-25, S-LC-26]

**Knuckout (salon plus academy, Chandigarh/Zirakpur/Ludhiana) and Headmasters (Mohali/Ludhiana).** Salon chains that use client flow and brand-product partnerships to feed academies. Their main edge is physical reach into Zirakpur, Mohali and Ludhiana. Neither publishes fees. Implication: they set expectations of "learn on real models and brides"; PNA's studio can offer the same. [S-LC-17, S-LC-18, S-LC-38]

**Nav Brar Studios & Makeup Academy (founder-led, Mohali/Chandigarh).** The best model to study. One-month pro course in small batches, short high-ticket-feeling courses (4-day self-makeup at INR 15,000), online masterclasses, cohort launches announced via press (Wedding Punjab, ANI/PNN), and sponsored profiles in Forbes India (2020) and The Tribune (2022). Enquiry runs through WhatsApp and Instagram DM. Fees for the flagship course are not public. Implication: PNA can match the format cheaply; the differentiator would be proof (reviews, outcomes) and published pricing. [CLAIM; S-LC-27 to S-LC-33]

**Tanvi KG Makeup (founder-led, Chandigarh).** Celebrity and film positioning (named Bollywood clients, "Hollywood" credit), a two-week certified course, teen and personal-makeup courses, and a prominent "Google Verified 4.9 / 125 reviews" badge on her site. Prices on request only. The page contradicts itself on years of experience and start date. Implication: a single visible rating badge is a strong trust device; PNA should audit its own Google rating first. [CLAIM; S-LC-34]

**Government-aided options (MCM DAV, HMV Jalandhar).** One-year diplomas at INR 3,730 and INR 35,000 respectively, affiliated to Panjab University / GNDU. They address a different need (formal credential; degree-holders for MCM DAV) but anchor "low price" perceptions for parents. [S-LC-41, S-LC-42]

**Others with thin data.** Abhishek Makeovers, Niti Luthra, Parthi Chopra's The Academy, SS Makeup Academy, Gagan Gandhi (Sector 8-C, permanent makeup) and AGE International (Ludhiana) show up in directories or press but with no usable fees or course specifics. SS Makeup Academy's website is down (hosting suspended). Treat all as monitored, not profiled.
