# National Competitor Landscape: Indian Makeup and Beauty Education

**Project:** Priyanka Nanda Makeup Academy growth strategy · **Workstream:** National competitive intelligence (Gate A) · **Prepared:** 2026-10-16 · **Scope:** national players only; Chandigarh-local players are in `research/chandigarh_competitors.md`.

**Tag key.** **[FACT]** = observed on a registry, a dated publication, or a competitor's own page as a *published* price or statement of what it offers (the observation is verified; the underlying boast may not be). **[CLAIM]** = competitor's self-reported or unaudited assertion (student counts, placements, accreditation, awards). **[EST]** = our estimate, arithmetic shown. **[HYP]** = hypothesis to test. Source IDs (S-NC-xx) are in `research/source_fragments/sources_national_competitors.md`; all accessed 2026-10-16.

**How to read the evidence.** Fetched pages were summarised by a small extraction model, so exact figures should be re-checked on the live page before they go into a client deck. Most chain sites hide fees; where a number comes from an aggregator or a search snippet it is labelled and should not be quoted as the competitor's price.

## 1. Executive summary

1. **Scale in this market is franchise-built.** Lakmē Academy powered by Aptech claims 140+ centres [CLAIM, S-NC-01]; Orane claims 110+ to 120+ centres [CLAIM, S-NC-15, S-NC-16]; VLCC claims 106+ institutes [CLAIM, S-NC-13]. None of these figures is independently audited and each brand's own pages disagree with themselves. Franchise investment is quoted at roughly INR 30-60 lakh [CLAIM/FR, S-NC-07, S-NC-19, S-NC-21; low reliability].
2. **Fee opacity is the norm.** Of the 10 players whose own web properties I could read, only Pearl, Daniel Bauer, AAFT Online and one Jawed Habib Delhi centre publish a price on a course page [FACT, S-NC-22, S-NC-26, S-NC-42, S-NC-31]. Lakmē's prices surface through press releases, not its centre sites; VLCC, Orane (centres), Meribindiya, Enrich and Naturals publish none. Aggregators fill the gap with unsourced, conflicting ranges.
3. **Observed price ladder (list prices):** micro/digital INR 499-2,999; short modules about INR 35,000-50,000; core 3-6 month professional programmes about INR 80,000-1.5 lakh; premium celebrity/design-school programmes INR 2.25-5.95 lakh (Daniel Bauer, ex GST) and INR 4.45 lakh (Pearl, incl. GST). See section 5.1.
4. **Premium tier is thinly populated and sells access, not syllabus.** Daniel Bauer (3 to 4 metros, INR 2.25-5.95 lakh) and Pearl (INR 4.45 lakh) are the only players I found with published premium prices; their pitch is backstage access, industry placements and brand halo [CLAIM, S-NC-26, S-NC-27].
5. **The winning acquisition model is counsellor-led conversion with financing.** Every serious player runs a lead form plus callback or demo class, and nearly all attach EMI or loans (Orane 0% EMI via Bajaj Finance/LoanTap/SaveIN; Pearl no-cost EMI via GrayQuest; Lakmē student loan; DBA EMI) [FACT, S-NC-15, S-NC-22, S-NC-04, S-NC-26]. Heavy discount anchoring ("up to 50% off", "Buy 1 Get 3" at INR 34,999 vs INR 1,10,000) is displayed on the sites [FACT, S-NC-04, S-NC-31]; that realised prices sit well below list is our inference [HYP].
6. **Certification is the main trust currency, and only part of it is verifiable.** The CIDESCO directory independently lists 5 Orane centres (including Chandigarh IN543 and Jalandhar IN785), 2 VLCC Delhi institutes, Enrich Mumbai and one Lakmē centre (Pimpri, Pune) [FACT, S-NC-09]. NSDC/B&WSSC/ISO claims could not be checked against registries [CLAIM]. Daniel Bauer, Meribindiya, Jawed Habib, Naturals and Pearl do not appear in CIDESCO's India list [FACT, S-NC-09].
7. **"100% placement" is universal and therefore meaningless.** VLCC, Orane, Jawed Habib and Meribindiya all assert 100% placement or assistance with no cohort data; Lakmē's Noida site states placement is not guaranteed [FACT, S-NC-04]. A founder-led academy that publishes verifiable outcomes would be unusual.
8. **Orane is the national player that matters most in Chandigarh.** It operates a Sector 34A centre, per-city microsites (Chandigarh, Ludhiana, Jalandhar, Dehradun) in local languages, a CIDESCO-listed Chandigarh school, and a national online arm that uses INR 499 celebrity masterclasses (Ojas Rajani) as a funnel [FACT, S-NC-15, S-NC-10, S-NC-17, S-NC-19].
9. **Celebrity-led academies are fewer than the brief assumed.** I could verify a real academy business only for Daniel Bauer. Bharat & Dorris (BDHMA, 2019 interview), Namrata Soni (undated "school"), Parul Garg (aggregator fee only) and Anu Kaushik (WeddingWire mention) are thinly evidenced. Mickey Contractor appears as an INR 2,999 online course, Ojas Rajani via Orane, and I found no academy for Elton Fernandez or Corinne Adams [S-NC-45 to S-NC-48]. International brands (MAC, Kryolan, London School of Makeup) show no verified India academy offering.
10. **White space [HYP]:** (a) transparent pricing in the INR 1.5-2.25 lakh band that sits between chains and premium academies; (b) verified, cohort-level outcomes; (c) founder-taught small-batch bridal specialisation for North India; (d) online-to-offline hybrid built around a low-ticket masterclass funnel. Details in section 6.

## 4. Player profiles

For each: positioning, strengths, weaknesses, implication. Reviews: **Google rating and review counts were not retrievable for any player** (see section 8). Instagram/YouTube: **Instagram fetch blocked (HTTP 429)**; no follower counts are recorded and none should be read as business success.

### 4.1 Academy chains and brands

**Lakmē Academy powered by Aptech**
- *Positioning/target:* mass-premium beauty career school with the Lakmē brand halo; 10th-pass entry; foundation and advanced tracks in makeup, hair, skin, nails, cosmetology [CLAIM, S-NC-02, S-NC-04].
- *Footprint/model:* "140+ centres" [CLAIM, S-NC-01]. Operated through Aptech (listed company; group revenue INR 46,010 lakh FY25, no Lakmē segment figure [S-NC-08, snippet]). A franchise directory quotes 80-160 franchisees and INR 30-60 lakh investment, but that page is internally inconsistent [S-NC-07, low]. Centres run their own domains (Noida, Bangalore, Indore), which suggests franchisee-operated local marketing.
- *Portfolio/fees:* the two fees we can source come from press releases: INR 1.3-1.5 lakh for 176 hrs (Nov 2024, also syndicated by Business Standard/ANI, S-NC-03) and "from INR 47,000" for 64 hrs (Jun 2025) [CLAIM, S-NC-02, S-NC-01]. The Indore centre site lists course families and an "International Pathway Program" but no prices or operator name [FACT, S-NC-06]. Centre sites show no prices but advertise "Fee Discount - Upto 50% Off" and student loans [FACT, S-NC-04].
- *Trust signals:* centre sites claim B&WSSC and CIDESCO; the CIDESCO directory confirms one Lakmē centre only [FACT, S-NC-09]. Awards cited are 2018-2020 [CLAIM, S-NC-01]. Placement: "assistance", with an explicit not-guaranteed disclaimer on the Noida site [FACT, S-NC-04].
- *Funnel:* free demo-class and counsellor-call forms, callback, WhatsApp [FACT, S-NC-04, S-NC-05].
- *Chandigarh presence:* a Chandigarh centre could not be verified.
- *Strengths:* brand recognition, breadth, loan/EMI, modular short courses. *Weaknesses:* inconsistent local sites, no published prices, claims vary by centre.
- *Implication:* a trust-anchored, discount-led competitor for mid-market bridal and career seekers; price-matching is a losing game.

**VLCC Institute**
- *Positioning:* wellness/beauty/nutrition institute with a government-skilling story; Class 8 entry for beauty, Class 12 for nutrition [CLAIM, S-NC-11].
- *Footprint:* "106+ institutes", 1 lakh+ trained since 2001, Tier-2/3 focus [CLAIM, PR, S-NC-13]; own site says 100+ institutes, 75+ cities, 15K students per year [CLAIM, S-NC-11]. The counts cannot be reconciled from public pages.
- *Portfolio/fees:* 100+ courses across nine categories; short certificates 1-3 months, diplomas 6-12 months [CLAIM, S-NC-11]. Fees not published; aggregator values (INR 78,000-1.66 lakh for a 3-month Gurgaon makeup course) are unsourced [S-NC-14, low].
- *Trust signals:* NSDC/B&WSSC alignment and CIDESCO mentioned [CLAIM]; two VLCC Delhi institutes are in the CIDESCO directory [FACT, S-NC-09]. "100% guaranteed placement for eligible students" and "300+ hiring partners" have no outcome data [CLAIM, S-NC-11, S-NC-13].
- *Funnel:* OTP-verified form, state/city/institute picker, "counsellor will call within 24 hours", toll-free number, free demo classes [FACT, S-NC-11].
- *Strengths:* institutional credibility, placement-partner narrative, scholarships up to 100% [CLAIM]. *Weaknesses:* 24-hour callback promise is slow by WhatsApp standards; generalist.
- *Implication:* competes for the safe-career student and parent; a founder-led academy can beat the response time and personal attention, not the institutional signal.

**Orane International**
- *Positioning:* "India's leading beauty and wellness academy" with CIDESCO/NSDC messaging; career-skill training, 10th-pass entry, weekend/fast-track batches [CLAIM, S-NC-15].
- *Footprint:* 110+ campuses and 80,000+ students [CLAIM, S-NC-15] versus 120+ centres in 22 states and "100,000 success stories since 2009" [CLAIM, S-NC-16]; franchise minimum investment INR 40 lakh per Orane [SNIP, S-NC-19], INR 30-50 lakh per Franchise India [FR, S-NC-21].
- *Chandigarh:* Orane Signature Academy, Sector 34A (the page gives two different floor/building addresses), Mon-Sat 9:30-6:00; CIDESCO directory lists the same school (IN543, programmes "Beauty Therapy, Make-up") [FACT, S-NC-10]. Ludhiana and Jalandhar microsites also exist (Jalandhar CIDESCO IN785) [S-NC-09, S-NC-19].
- *Portfolio/fees:* makeup, hair, aesthetics, nail art, combo cosmetology, B.Voc [CLAIM]. Centre pages do not publish fees. Orane states a programme range of INR 6,500-4,00,000 with 0% EMI [SNIP, S-NC-19]. Online: INR 499 two-hour Ojas Rajani masterclass (21 Jul 2025) [FACT, S-NC-17]; online bridal makeup/hair and 6-month diploma at INR 80,000 [SNIP]. Propelld shows approximate bands (certificate INR 25-35k, diploma 35-65k, advanced 65k-1 lakh) which conflict with Orane's own online prices, so in-centre pricing is unresolved [S-NC-20, low].
- *Funnel:* free counselling form (name, phone, email, course), enrol/view-courses/directions buttons, 0% EMI via Bajaj Finance, LoanTap, SaveIN [FACT, S-NC-15]; free demo and trial class on the Janakpuri site [S-NC-18]; "Enroll free, sit through a class & decide" online [S-NC-16]. Local-language microsites per city [FACT from search titles, S-NC-19].
- *Strengths:* only chain with a CIDESCO-listed school in Chandigarh and in Punjab, strong local SEO structure, online funnel with celebrity masterclasses. *Weaknesses:* fee opacity, inconsistent self-reported numbers, centre addresses inconsistent on its own page, generic testimonials without outcomes [S-NC-15].
- *Implication:* the benchmark rival in the PNA catchment (see also local report). Differentiate on founder expertise, batch size and published outcomes.

**Pearl Academy (makeup certificate)**
- *Positioning:* design-school brand for fashion-career students; Fashion & Celebrity Makeup & Hair, 11 months [FACT, S-NC-22].
- *Fees/financing:* INR 4,44,778 incl. GST, annual increase clause; 6-month no-cost EMI (GrayQuest) or 10-month EMI; Axis/IDFC/Yes finance partners; scholarships via aptitude interview [FACT, S-NC-22, S-NC-23]. Pearl's own blog quotes INR 1.5-2.5 lakh average for 11-month certificates, which conflicts with its fee page [S-NC-25]; we rely on the fee page.
- *Footprint:* Delhi-West, Delhi-South, Bengaluru, Mumbai, Jaipur [FACT, S-NC-22]. No Chandigarh campus.
- *Funnel:* brochure form with OTP, helpline, WhatsApp, structured admission cycle [S-NC-24].
- *Strengths:* transparent fee, structured finance, brand. *Weaknesses:* price and metro-only footprint exclude most Punjab/Haryana learners; makeup is a small part of the catalogue.
- *Implication:* sets the upper price anchor; low direct threat in Chandigarh.

**Jawed Habib Academy**
- *Positioning:* hair-led brand extending into makeup and beauty; low-price, high-discount [S-NC-31].
- *Footprint/model:* brand site shows an Andheri (Mumbai) address and a franchise sign-up; a separate Laxmi Nagar (Delhi) site publishes prices [S-NC-31, S-NC-32]. Franchise terms from Smergers are snippet-only [S-NC-34]; an aggregator course list for the Delhi academy (four certificate courses, last updated Oct 2023, no fees) is stale [S-NC-33].
- *Fees:* Complete Makeup Course "35K"; beauty bundle 50K; "Buy 1 Get 3" at INR 34,999 versus INR 1,10,000 and "Flat 60% off" banners [FACT as displayed, S-NC-31]. Discount mechanics are inconsistent across banners (Get 2, 3, 4).
- *Trust claims:* "Globally recognized", "100% placement assistance guarantee", none verified [CLAIM].
- *Implication:* sets the low-price floor (about INR 35k) for makeup training in the NCR; heavy discounting trains buyers to expect deals. Unverified allegations about the founder appeared in one search summary (2025); not relied upon and should be checked against primary news before any use.

**Naturals Academy / Naturals Beauty Academy**
- *Positioning:* salon-chain training arm (Training Academy 2008, Beauty Academy 2021 per a franchise listing, snippet only) [S-NC-38]; courses named include Diploma in Cosmetology, Advanced Makeup Artistry, Bridal Makeup and Hairstyling [S-NC-36].
- *Evidence:* the 2025 trade piece gives 825+ Naturals salons but no centre count, durations or fees [S-NC-35]; Naturals' own site has no academy page [S-NC-39]. Aggregator shows a 6-month Mylapore course at INR 88,000 [SNIP, S-NC-37].
- *Implication:* a South-India-centred brand with unverified North presence; low priority for Chandigarh.

**Enrich Academy**
- *Positioning:* West-India salon chain's academy; the only chain in our set where CIDESCO listing is explicit and Mumbai-specific [FACT, S-NC-09].
- *Evidence:* salon site (114 salons, 7 cities by our tally) has no academy pages [S-NC-40]. No fees found.
- *Implication:* low relevance to North India.

**Meribindiya International Academy**
- *Positioning:* Delhi/Noida makeup and cosmetology academy; "India's No 1 Makeup Academy", small classes, internships, "Learn with Earn", loan facility [CLAIM, S-NC-30].
- *Claims:* 5,000+ students placed, 100% placement, 12+ awards (bodies unnamed), ISO Certified and NSDC Approved [CLAIM, S-NC-30].
- *Fees:* not published; aggregator range INR 80,000-2.5 lakh for 3-5 month diploma and INR 2.5-5 lakh for master's in cosmetology [S-NC-50, low].
- *Funnel:* Call Me Back, Book a Demo, enquiry, WhatsApp, online registration [FACT, S-NC-30] - the richest lead-capture stack among independents.
- *Implication:* the closest functional peer to a founder-led academy in NCR; its playbook (WhatsApp, demo, loan, "placement" language) is the template PNA will be compared against.

**Shahnaz Husain Beauty Academy, Toni&Guy Academy, Euro Chroma, Blossom Kochhar (brief)**
- Shahnaz Husain: herbal/Ayurvedic diplomas (herbal beauty therapy, aromatherapy, spa) per a trade roundup; Delhi listing only; fees not found; directory page unusable [S-NC-36, S-NC-54, very low].
- Toni&Guy: hair-first; Indore academy launched May 2026 with 12-18 week beginner programme; no makeup course confirmed, no fees, no external accreditation named [S-NC-41].
- Euro Chroma (Lajpat Nagar): Kryolan-supported and VTCT-accredited by its own claim; 6-week Certificate in Professional Makeup; listing last updated 2019, so possibly stale [S-NC-52, low].
- Blossom Kochhar College: CIDESCO-listed Delhi school (IN464) [FACT, S-NC-09]; older Tribune item says IMA-affiliated, referencing London School of Make-up as an IMA member [S-NC-53, low].

### 4.2 Celebrity and educator-led academies (verification status)

**Daniel Bauer Academy** (verified, medium-high evidence)
- *Positioning:* celebrity hair-and-makeup artist brand; "IIM of the makeup industry", fashion-week and celebrity-wedding credits, backstage access [CLAIM, S-NC-26, S-NC-27]. Operated by AFI Creative LLP [FACT, S-NC-26, S-NC-29].

### 5.2 Acquisition model (what appears to win)

1. **Lead form + counsellor callback + demo class** is universal among chains (VLCC, Orane, Lakmē, Meribindiya) [FACT, S-NC-11, S-NC-15, S-NC-04, S-NC-30]. Speed varies: VLCC promises a call "within 24 hours".
2. **WhatsApp and phone as conversion rails:** Meribindiya, DBA, Jawed Habib and Pearl all expose WhatsApp [FACT]. Supports project hypothesis H6 (counselling-led WhatsApp conversion), though we have no conversion data.
3. **Financing removes the price barrier:** 0% EMI (Orane), no-cost EMI (Pearl), loans (Lakmē, Meribindiya), EMI (DBA) [FACT].
4. **Local presence at scale:** Orane city microsites, Lakmē centre domains; likely supports local search capture [HYP; SERP not systematically audited].
5. **Low-ticket live masterclass as lead magnet:** Orane's INR 499 two-hour Ojas Rajani session with an enrol-free-first online model [FACT, S-NC-16, S-NC-17].
6. **Trust stack = certification + awards + placement language + parent brand,** rather than reviews or outcomes data. No player published cohort-level outcomes.
7. **Fee transparency is a differentiator, not a norm** (only Pearl, DBA, AAFT Online, one JH centre).

### 5.3 Franchise and operating models

- Lakmē/Aptech, Orane, VLCC, Jawed Habib, Naturals are franchise or licence networks; franchise investment quotes: Lakmē INR 30-60 lakh (conflicting, low reliability), Orane INR 40 lakh minimum (Orane) / INR 30-50 lakh (Franchise India), Naturals Academy INR 20-30 lakh, Jawed Habib Academy a small-format listing (snippet) [S-NC-07, S-NC-19, S-NC-21, S-NC-38, S-NC-34]. - [HYP] Franchise growth needs a standardised curriculum, brand and counselling script; a founder-brand academy would have to separate the founder's teaching from the curriculum to franchise (links to H3).

## 6. White-space hypotheses for a North India founder-led academy

All **[HYP]**: to be tested in customer research and by mystery shopping.

| ID | Hypothesis | Evidence basis | How to test |
|---|---|---|---|
| WS1 | **Publish transparent prices and payment plans.** Chains hide fees; only Pearl, DBA, AAFT Online publish. A clear fee page with EMI could win trust and rank for fee queries now served by aggregators. | 4 of 10 players publish; aggregator ranges conflict (e.g. VLCC INR 78k vs 1.66 lakh) | Landing-page A/B on fee transparency; track enquiry quality |
| WS2 | **Occupy the INR 1.5-2.25 lakh band** with a founder-taught small-batch professional programme (4-6 months). | Gap between T2 (up to about 1.5 lakh) and T3 (2.25 lakh+) | Price testing, willingness-to-pay interviews, competitor mystery-shop |
| WS3 | **Verifiable outcomes instead of "100% placement".** Publish batch-level data (portfolio completion, bookings, internship partners, consented testimonials). | Universal unsupported placement claims; Lakmē Noida admits no guarantee | Pilot a one-page outcomes report for the next batch |
| WS4 | **Named external certification** (e.g. a specific international body) rather than generic NSDC/ISO language. | Only Orane (Chandigarh, Jalandhar) among North-India players is CIDESCO-listed; Pearl/DBA rely on own certificates | Check feasibility, cost and exam model with certifier; test conversion effect |
| WS5 | **Bridal/North-Indian specialisation with business skills** (pricing, client handling, social media) for freelance artists, not only salon jobs. | National chains pitch broad cosmetology and salon placements; DBA sells fashion/editorial; Orane Online pitches "freelance career" | Customer interviews on career intent; compare enquiry mix |
| WS6 | **Hybrid funnel:** INR 499-2,999 live masterclass -> offline cohort, with founder as the draw. | Orane's INR 499 masterclass and "enrol free, sit through a class" show the pattern, but with celebrity guests, not founders; no hybrid certificate program found | Pilot two masterclasses and measure masterclass-to-counselling conversion |
| WS7 | **Speed-to-lead advantage:** WhatsApp response in minutes vs VLCC's 24-hour call promise. | VLCC "within 24 hours" [S-NC-11]; supports H6/H7 | Mystery-shop competitor response times (not done) |
| WS8 | **Destination-learning pitch for Punjab/Haryana/HP/J&K** with accommodation/travel support. | National chains are centre-led with local intake; DBA/Pearl are metro destinations | Test with out-of-tricity ad sets and enquiry origin analysis |

### Implications for a Chandigarh founder-led academy [HYP]

- Do not compete with Orane/VLCC/Jawed Habib on discount depth; their list-price opacity and franchise scale make price wars unwinnable. Compete on founder access, small batches, transparent pricing and named certification.
- DBA proves premium founder pricing exists, but its proof points (fashion weeks, celebrity weddings) are metro-based; PNA should evidence regional bridal/editorial outcomes it can actually document.
- Orane's Sector 34A school with CIDESCO listing and local-language funnel is the benchmark to match on trust signals and funnel speed.
- Key founder-dependency risk (H3): all the celebrity examples (DBA, Mickey Contractor, Ojas via Orane) monetise the person; scalable players (Orane, Lakmē, VLCC) monetise a curriculum plus brand. A plan to scale beyond one centre must decide which model PNA follows.

## 8. Gaps and limitations

1. **Search budget exhausted** (200 WebSearch calls per turn, shared by all agents) before final gap-fill. Not located: official sites for Parul Garg, Namrata Soni, Bharat & Dorris; other North-India celebrity MUA academies; YouTube educators with paid courses; beauty-education apps.
2. **WebSearch is US-only and was not used as a SERP audit.** Visibility notes are indicative: Orane's Chandigarh pages surfaced for Chandigarh-intent queries; a Lakmē Chandigarh page did not; aggregators (Shiksha, Collegedunia, Careers360, Promilo, FindMyCollege) dominate fee queries and are often stale [observation from search results only].
3. **Blocked/failed sources:** lakmeacademy.com (503), orane.com main (AWS WAF challenge), Instagram (429), maccosmetics.in and several aggregators (403), parulgarg.com / shahnazhusain / londonschoolofmakeup (DNS failure/503). Orane centre fees and Lakmē brand-site content were therefore not seen first-hand. Full log in the sources file.
4. **No Google ratings/review counts and no Instagram/YouTube follower counts** for any player; social posture is not assessed. Followers would be indicative only in any case.
5. **Accreditation (NSDC, B&WSSC, ISO, CII, VTCT, CIBTAC) not verified** against registries; only the CIDESCO directory was checked, and its listing does not specify which programmes are examined.
6. **Outcome claims (placement, students trained, centres) are self-reported** and often internally inconsistent (e.g. Orane 110+ vs 120+ centres; VLCC 100+ vs 106+; DBA course naming and class counts).
7. **Fees are list prices.** Discounts, scholarships, GST treatment and kit inclusion differ; aggregator values are unsourced and conflict. Re-verify any figure on the live page before quoting.
8. **Extraction risk:** page content was summarised by a model, not read as raw HTML; verify key figures manually.
9. **Time sensitivity:** most pages are undated; the DBA course page differs between a search-result snapshot and the fetch.
10. **Chandigarh footprint** of Lakmē, VLCC, Jawed Habib and Naturals not verified; the local-competition agent should confirm.
11. **Not done:** mystery shopping (response times, counsellor scripts, realised prices), Meta Ad Library review of competitor ads (a connected Meta Ads tool exists but runs through the user's ad account and was outside this brief; recommended as a next step with the user's consent), B&WSSC/NSDC registry checks, SERP position tracking for "makeup course in Chandigarh/Ludhiana/Delhi".
12. **Reputational items:** one search summary mentioned 2025 allegations involving the founder of a profiled brand; unverified and excluded from conclusions.
