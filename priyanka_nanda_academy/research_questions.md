# Research Questions and Founder Inputs

## Core questions (from brief)
1. How large is the realistic addressable opportunity in Chandigarh/Tricity, North India, and nationally?
2. Is Chandigarh alone an adequate market? Can it attract students from surrounding areas?
3. Who are the major competitors, what do they sell, and how do they acquire students?
4. Should scale come from one flagship, multi-centre, online, hybrid, a services marketplace, or adjacent revenue streams?
5. What should the brand stand for and whom should it target?
6. What is the correct admissions funnel and channel mix?
7. What operating economics and funding are needed?
8. What should happen in the next 90 days, 12 months and three years?

## Open founder questions (do not block; models use labelled assumptions)
1. Exact enrolments by course for the last 12–24 months, and fee charged per course (incl. discounts).
2. Current revenue split: courses vs. the founder's own bridal/makeup services vs. kits/products.
3. Current premises: owned/rented, size (sq ft), rent, maximum students per batch, number of practice stations.
4. Team: trainers/assistants/counsellors today, pay structure.
5. Enquiry volumes per month and source (Instagram DM, WhatsApp, referrals, walk-ins), and how many convert.
6. Certification offered today (own certificate, B&WSSC/NSDC, international bodies).
7. Outcome evidence: alumni working as MUAs, alumni earnings/bookings, placements.
8. Assets: Instagram handle(s), website/domain, Google Business Profile access, any content library.
9. Founder's own bridal-services business: will it continue alongside the academy?
10. Appetite for franchising vs. company-owned centres; willingness to hire a general manager/admissions head.
