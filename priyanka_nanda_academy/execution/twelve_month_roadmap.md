## 0. Summary

1. **The year has one job:** prove the Chandigarh Flagship in today's studio on Tranche T1, then build it out only if G1 is green on May–July actuals. Meanwhile the founder's teaching share falls from about 100% to 40%, and her teaching hours are budgeted, not hidden (`recommended_strategy.md` §1).
2. **Four quarters, four outcomes** [REC]:
   - **Q1 Nov–Jan: instrument and start.**
     - The Admissions & Operations Lead is in post on 2 Nov.
     - CRM and SLA, GBP, five core pages, and fees with the all-in cost.
     - Lead Trainer from 1 Dec; **cohort 1 on Mon 4 Jan**.
     - **G0 on Fri 15 Jan 2027**; tests T1/T2 from 18 Jan.
   - **Q2 Feb–Apr: prove, inside the T1 cap.**
     - Outcomes Report (31 Jan) and ≥30 reviews by 26 Feb.
     - The deferred Q1 items: SEO pages, interview wave 2, mystery shop, tests T3/T5, PNA Method v1.
     - **KT-1a cut review on Fri 5 Mar.** Cohort 2 on 1 Mar; weekend/evening studio slot from 6 Mar.
     - **Lead Trainer certified on Fri 9 Apr.** Cohort 3 on 26 Apr.
   - **Q3 May–Jul: run the peak in the studio and read G1.**
     - Seats bind (78 / 67 / 77% of demand fits). Cohort 4 on 21 Jun, the first month the station test can pass.
     - Heads of terms are negotiated *subject to G1*.
     - **G1 on Mon 26 Jul.** If green: lease, deposit and fit-out that week.
   - **Q4 Aug–Oct: build and extend (Tranche T2).**
     - Move to a 12-station academy. Second trainer, second counsellor, Assistant #2 and front desk from 2 Aug.
     - Price step 1 from the 16 Aug intake. Media ₹80k → ₹1.0L.
     - **Destination pilot of 6 students** (16 Aug and 20 Sep intakes). Pop-ups in Ludhiana (Sep) and Patiala (Oct).
     - **KT-10 post-move watch. Year-1 review Fri 29 Oct.**
3. **Budget by quarter, planning case [EST, `Staged_G1M9`; methodology §1A]:**
   - **Outflow** (opex + capex): ₹14.7L / ₹14.3L / ₹30.0L / ₹21.5L = **₹80.6L**.
   - **Revenue:** ₹5.5L / ₹13.4L / ₹16.6L / ₹25.0L = **₹60.6L**.
   - **EBITDA:** −₹6.7L / −₹1.0L / +₹1.3L / +₹3.5L = **−₹2.8L** (−₹11.8L after a ₹75k/month founder-role salary).
   - **Cash used inside T1: ₹11.8L** (the high is reached in April and held to the G1 month; T1 cap ₹15L).
   - **Peak cash need: ₹25.2L in July**, the fit-out month. That leaves ₹4.8L of headroom on the ₹30L planned line and ₹9.8L on the ₹35L authorised.
   - **81 career enrolments.**
4. **What can be lost, honestly [EST, §1A.2]:**
   - Funnel fails and nobody acts: **≈₹36L** by Oct 2027, visible Feb 2027.
   - Funnel fails and KT-1a is applied on 5 March: **≈₹25L**.
   - False-positive G1 and a failed peak season: **≈₹33L**, visible Sep 2027. Of this, ₹11.6L of fit-out is sunk; the ₹3.1L deposit is recoverable.
   - **Upside** (G1 at M6): ₹66.7L revenue, −₹0.8L EBITDA, peak ₹26.5L.
5. **Hiring:**
   - **T1:** Admissions & Operations Lead and Assistant #1 (Nov). Lead Trainer, shooter, Content Executive and performance freelancer (Dec). SEO freelancer (Feb). Retainer senior trainer as cover (Feb).
   - **T2 (from Aug 2027):** second trainer, second counsellor (0.5 FTE), Assistant #2 and front-desk coordinator.
   - **Year 2:** Academy Manager from Nov 2027 (M13).
6. **The hidden constraint is founder hours, not share.** On the planning case her modelled teaching runs 59–103 h a month to July, then **114 h in August** and ≥106 h through October. If G1 lands at M6 it peaks at **≈128 h in May**. Masterclasses, assessment, recording and the studio are on top of this. If she exceeds 110 h for two consecutive months, the second trainer is pulled forward, or takes more slots once in post (D6a).
