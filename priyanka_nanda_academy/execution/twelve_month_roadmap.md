## 0. Summary

1. **The year has one job: turn a founder-taught, on-demand class into a trainer-delivered flagship that fills cohorts, publishes proof and pays for itself, while the founder's teaching share falls from about 100% to 30%** (A-BM-10; CA-04).
2. **Four quarters, four outcomes** [REC]:
   - **Q1 Nov–Jan: fix the leaks.** Admissions desk, CRM and tracking, GBP, fee page, lead trainer, PNA Method v1, tests at ₹40–60k a month. **G0 on 15 Jan 2027.**
   - **Q2 Feb–Apr: prove and decide.** Test read-outs, lead trainer certified, ≥30 reviews, Outcomes Report out. **Scale-and-space checkpoint on 5 Mar 2027**: media ramp and the academy lease. **Proof gate on 30 Apr 2027**: price step and the Destination go/no-go.
   - **Q3 May–Jul: scale into the S1 peak.** New 12-station academy (May), L3 at ₹90k list / ₹80k realised, media ₹1.0L a month, first EBITDA-positive month (May), first regional pop-up (Jul).
   - **Q4 Aug–Oct: prove the flagship.** Destination pilot cohort (Aug), second pop-up (Sep), **G1 decision on 29 Oct 2027**, Year-2 hires and plan. G2 (regional engine) follows in Jan–Apr 2028.
3. **Base budget by quarter [EST, `Outlook_Base`]:** total outflow (opex + capex) ₹13.8L / ₹29.0L / ₹22.8L / ₹19.9L = **₹85.6L**, against revenue ₹5.7L / ₹12.8L / ₹26.7L / ₹26.6L = **₹71.8L**. EBITDA −₹6.7L / −₹1.4L / +₹7.4L / +₹6.7L = **+₹6.0L**. **Peak cash need ₹25.7L at end-April**, inside the ₹30L envelope with ₹4.3L headroom.
4. **Hiring (Base):** counsellor, assistant and shooter in Nov; **lead trainer 1 Dec**; content executive and performance freelancer in Dec; SEO freelancer in Jan; second counsellor (0.5 FTE) in Apr; second assistant and front-desk coordinator in May; **trainer #2 and academy manager in Nov 2027 after G1** (A-ECO-52..57, -124..-126; `3yr_Scenarios`).
5. **Capacity:** 8 stations × 2 slots (14.4 usable seats) until April, then 12 stations × 2.5 slots (27 seats) from May. Demand exceeds seats in April and July in the model, so the space decision cannot wait for G1 (`Outlook_Base` row 28).
6. **The hidden constraint:** the founder's *share* falls on plan, but her *hours* do not, because volume triples. Model-derived founder teaching is ≈66–81 h a month in Q1 and **peaks at ≈123 h in May** before easing to ≈79 h in October [EST, §7]. Hours fall to ≈38 h a month only in Year 2. The founder must budget for this, or fund trainer #2 earlier from the reserve.
