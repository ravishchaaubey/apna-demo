## 0. Summary

1. **The year has one job:** prove the Chandigarh Flagship on today's studio and the Tranche-T1 budget, then build it out at G1, while the founder's teaching share falls from about 100% to 40% (`recommended_strategy.md` §1).
2. **Four quarters, four outcomes** [REC]:
   - **Q1 Nov–Jan: fix the leaks.** Admissions desk, CRM and tracking, GBP, fees published at today's fee, Lead Trainer, PNA Method v1, first structured 12-week cohort (4 Jan). **G0 on Fri 15 Jan 2027**; paid tests T1/T2 start the following Monday.
   - **Q2 Feb–Apr: prove.** Outcomes Report (31 Jan), ≥30 Google reviews, Lead Trainer certified, second cohort trainer-led, tests read out, weekend/evening studio slot from March. **SG1–SG5 formal review Fri 5 Mar.** **G1 review 1 on Fri 9 Apr (M6, planning case)**: if green, Tranche T2 is released and the fit-out starts.
   - **Q3 May–Jul: build.** Move to a 12-station academy (3 May); price step 1 to ₹75k list; media ₹80k → ₹1.0L; Destination pilot cohort Jun–Jul; first pop-up pilot (Jul). **G1 latest decision Fri 30 Jul (M9)**: the late case moves in August.
   - **Q4 Aug–Oct: run and prepare Year 2.** Destination format live; second pop-up (Sep); Outcomes Report #2 (Sep, needed for price step 2); **Year-1 review Fri 29 Oct**, including KT-10 if G1 is still red; Academy Manager from Nov 2027. G2 (regional engine) window: Jan–Apr 2028.
3. **Budget by quarter, planning case [EST, `Staged_G1M6`; methodology §1A]:** outflow (opex + capex) ₹14.3L / ₹28.6L / ₹18.9L / ₹19.8L = **₹81.6L**; revenue ₹5.5L / ₹13.4L / ₹23.7L / ₹23.9L = **₹66.5L**; EBITDA −₹6.2L / −₹0.5L / +₹4.8L / +₹4.1L = **+₹2.2L** (−₹6.9L after a ₹75k/month founder-role salary). **Cash used before G1: ₹11.0L** (Tranche-T1 cap ₹15L). **Peak cash need ₹25.6L at end-April**, leaving ₹4.4L of headroom on the ₹30L planned line (₹9.4L on the ₹35L authorised with the ₹5L timing-only contingency); the KT-11 warning line (₹28L) is not reached. The recorded hybrid library (₹3.5L) sits in Year 2 (Tranche T3).
4. **Late case (G1 at M9) [EST, `Staged_G1M9`]:** revenue ₹59.9L, EBITDA −₹0.4L, peak cash ₹23.8L (July), ≈11 fewer career enrolments (80 vs 91), because only 67–78% of May–July demand fits in the studio before the August move.
5. **Hiring:** T1: Admissions Counsellor and Assistant (Nov), Lead Trainer, shooter, Content Executive and performance freelancer (Dec), SEO freelancer (Jan). T2 (from G1): second counsellor (0.5 FTE), second assistant and front-desk coordinator (May in the planning case), second trainer (model need from Nov 2027; we recommend hiring in Aug 2027). Academy Manager from Nov 2027 (M13).
6. **The hidden constraint is founder hours, not share.** Because volume triples while her share only falls to 40%, model-derived founder teaching rises from 59–81 h a month in Nov–Feb to **≈128 h in May** and stays ≥106 h through October [EST, §7], overlapping the next bridal season. The founder must budget for this or bring the second trainer forward.

### 11.2 Curriculum codification and quality assurance

**The PNA Method package** (positioning D5; founder 40–60 h to codify [EST]):
1. **Module map** for L1, L2, L3 (all three formats) and L4.
2. **8–12 named signature techniques** (working names such as "PNA humid-weather bridal base" are [HYP]), each with a written standard, a 3–5-minute founder demonstration video and before/after reference images.
3. **Lesson plans** per session: objective, demonstration, guided practice, independent practice, check.
4. **Assessment rubric** per technique (4-point scale) and a **portfolio-day rubric**.
5. **Trainer guide** (how to teach each module; common faults) and a **student workbook**.
6. **Basic theory recordings** (₹1.0L, A-ECO-152, January) in Tranche T1, used for the Destination pilot's live-online pre-work; the **recorded hybrid pre-work library** (₹3.5L, A-ECO-129) in Tranche T3, after G2 (Year 2).

| Version | Date | Content | Approver |
|---|---|---|---|
| v0.5 | Sun 13 Dec 2026 | Core bridal modules: lesson plans + draft rubric | Founder |
| **v1** | **Fri 15 Jan 2027** | Full L2 and L3 module set, rubric, technique videos, basic theory recordings | Founder |
| v1.1 | Fri 30 Apr 2027 | Revisions from cohort 1 and the first trainer-led modules | Founder |
| v2 | Fri 30 Jul 2027 | Adds the live-online pre-work syllabus (50–70 h) and the 5–6-week intensive timetable, using the Destination pilot's lessons; the recorded library follows in Year 2 | Founder |
| Annual update | Fri 29 Oct 2027, then yearly | Techniques added or retired; rubric recalibrated | Founder |

**QA loop** [REC]:
- **Observation:** the founder observes one session per educator per month (live or recorded) against an observation checklist.
- **Assessment:** every L3 graduate is assessed at portfolio day by the founder through Year 1, with the Lead Trainer as second assessor. Target: the two agree within one rubric point on ≥90% of looks; disagreements trigger a calibration session.
- **Student voice:** mid-course pulse (week 4 of L3) and end-of-course NPS for every cohort; trainer-led vs founder-led comparison per cohort (G1: within 0.3/5).
- **Cohort close review** within two weeks of each cohort end: fill, completion, pass rate, NPS, refunds/drops, practice hours delivered vs published, complaints.
- **Quarterly internal audit** against §11.1; target audit score ≥85% (the G3b benchmark, used here as the Year-1 standard).
- **Change control:** curriculum changes only through founder approval, logged with the version number.

### 11.4 Student support, including out-of-town housing

**Support across the journey [REC]:**
- **Before enrolment:** S1 counselling with a parent present (target ≥60%); written fee, refund/transfer and EMI terms before any token; Saturday parent-visit slots; monthly online Parents & Students Info Session (Sunday 19:00).
- **Onboarding:** welcome pack, timetable, optional kit list, batch WhatsApp group, attendance and conduct rules, named grievance contact.
- **During the course:** same-day follow-up on absences; one-to-one check-in at mid-course; weekly parent update for out-of-town and under-18 students; remedial practice slot; portfolio day.
- **Grievances:** acknowledged within 24 hours, resolved within 7 days, logged and reviewed monthly.
- **After the course:** Pro Circle free for year 1 (A-GRO-09); outcome surveys at 30/90/180/365 days (tech FR-13); referral code; review ask; studio assisting gigs in season; alumni booking desk.
- **Under-18 students and leads** are "children" under the DPDP Act: verifiable parental consent before processing or marketing (tech §10).

**Out-of-town housing partners: vetting for women's safety** (`geographic_expansion.md` §5.1; A-BM-20; S-CHD-18/20) [REC]:

| Check | Standard |
|---|---|
| Premises | **Women-only** PG or hostel within 15 minutes of the academy |
| Legal | Estate Office registration or licence verified on paper (only 25 licensed PGs were claimed, S-CHD-18 [CLAIM]) |
| Fire safety | Fire-safety compliance, working extinguishers, two usable exits, no blocked stairways (a fatal PG fire is documented, S-CHD-20) |
| Security | CCTV in common areas; resident warden; curfew and visitor policy; police verification of owner and staff |
| Living | Meals included; cohort rate ₹10–14k a month incl. meals [HYP, A-BM-20]; **pass-through pricing, no PNA margin** |
| Agreement | Written partner agreement covering the standards above, a duty to report incidents to PNA within 1 hour, and PNA's right to inspect unannounced; counsel to advise on liability and insurance |
| Inspection | On-site inspection by two PNA staff (at least one woman) before signing; walk-through video for parents; re-inspection every quarter and before every Destination cohort |

**Student-side protocol:** arrival-day orientation; local-guardian form; emergency-contact card; cohort WhatsApp group with a PNA duty contact; classes end by about 6:30 pm; mid-course parent call; parent satisfaction survey (target ≥4.5/5).
**Incident protocol (KT-8):** any serious incident → parent informed at once, partner suspended immediately, independent review before the next cohort.
**Timeline:** search from 1 Feb 2027; 2–3 partners signed by 30 Apr 2027, before out-of-town students arrive for the May–Jul peak (25% of career enrolments, A-GRO-37) and the Jun–Jul Destination pilot; re-audit before the August Destination intake.
**Owner and cost:** Growth Lead until May, then the front-desk/operations coordinator; ₹2,000 co-ordination per Destination student (A-ECO-225).
