# Marketing Channel Plan

**Client:** Priyanka Nanda Makeup Academy (PNA), Chandigarh · **Workstream:** Growth & MarTech · **Prepared:** 2026-10-16 · **Agent:** Growth and MarTech Agent (Opus)
**Strategy source:** `strategy/growth_marketing_strategy.md` (workstreams, tests T1–T8, gates G1–G5, 12-month budget) · **Assumptions:** `strategy/admissions_funnel.md` §9 (A-GRO-xx).

**Tags:** [FACT] · [CLAIM] · [EST] · [HYP] · [REC]. All budgets are **[EST]** and all KPI targets are **[HYP]** until replaced with PNA data.

**How to read the budgets:**
- Monthly budgets are **steady-state (months 7–12, May–Oct 2027)** in three spend scenarios:
  - **C** = conservative, ≈₹40k media/month
  - **B** = base, ≈₹1.0L
  - **A** = aggressive, ≈₹2.0L, **only after gates G1–G5 are green**
- During Phase 0–1 (Nov 2026–Feb 2027), paid channels run at **test level** (§3).
- "Team" means the cost sits in the team lines of the 12-month budget (`growth_marketing_strategy.md` §10) rather than in the channel's media budget.

---

## 1. Summary

1. **Owned and earned channels come first** because they are cheap and PNA is invisible today (CA-07): Google Business Profile, website/SEO, the academy Instagram account, WhatsApp and referral.
2. **Paid channels are tests until the gates pass.** These are Meta CTWA, Google Search and workshop campaigns. Each has explicit test → scale criteria.
3. **Base steady-state media of ₹1.0L/month splits roughly:**

   | Bucket | Monthly |
   |---|---|
   | Meta career CTWA (tricity + regional) | ₹32k |
   | Workshops | ₹15k |
   | Masterclass launches | ₹8k |
   | Proof and retargeting | ₹18k |
   | Google Search (brand + course-intent + regional) | ₹22k |
   | Test reserve | ₹5k |

4. **The scarcest skills are not ad buying.** They are **counselling discipline** (SLA, parent handling) and **content production** (portfolio proof). Hire or train for those first.
