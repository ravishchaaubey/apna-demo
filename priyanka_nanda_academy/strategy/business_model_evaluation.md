## 0. Executive summary

1. **Baseline:** PNA is a bridal and hair studio with founder-taught, on-demand classes. It has fewer than 60 paying students a year (CA-01), unpublished fees and almost no public digital footprint (CA-07; digital maturity 3/10, S-LC-51). Academy revenue is about **₹12–45 lakh a year, base about ₹30 lakh [EST]**. That is about **4% of the tricity base market** (50 of 1,256 students; CA-05). On this model the founder cannot step back from teaching, because she is the product, the counsellor and the only trainer.
2. **Primary model [REC]: the "Hybrid Chandigarh Flagship".** This is a trainer-delivered, small-batch, bridal-specialist academy in Chandigarh built on a codified "PNA Method". Its parts:
   - A published product ladder: discovery workshop → self-makeup → professional bridal programme → advanced masterclasses.
   - Online pre-work for theory, with offline practice, assessment and portfolio work.
   - The founder's bridal studio used as the live practicum.
   - An alumni booking desk.
   - Optional B&WSSC-aligned certification.

   The founder's role moves from teacher to brand face, curriculum owner, masterclass lead and final assessor. Founder teaching falls to **≤30% of contact hours by month 12 and ≤10–15% by month 24–36** (A-BM-10). This package is the highest-scoring full-scale model under all three weightings: **3.85 / 3.90 / 3.85** on base, growth and founder weights (section 4).
3. **Optional second growth engine [REC]: "Regional destination learning".** This means compressed hybrid intensives in Chandigarh for students from Punjab, HP, J&K and Haryana. It comes with vetted women-only accommodation and a parents' orientation. Demand is fed by a rotating circuit of pop-up discovery workshops and founder masterclasses in Ludhiana, Jalandhar, Patiala, Amritsar and Shimla. It reaches a pool **26x the tricity** (12.4M vs 0.47M women aged 17–35; S-CHD-01) without centre capex. It is released only at Gate G2 (flagship fill ≥75% for two quarters, out-of-town enrolments ≥15%, pop-up attendee-to-enrolment ≥8%, trainer bench ≥3).
4. **Defer, with triggers:**
   - Second physical site: partner-hosted Ludhiana satellite first, an owned site only later (Gate G3a).
   - Franchise, licensing and train-the-trainer (Gate G3b).
   - Stand-alone national digital courses.
   - Pro membership.
   - Corporate/B2B grooming.
   - International credentials (CIDESCO/VTCT).
   - Delhi NCR in any physical form.
5. **Reject (3-year horizon):**
   - Full beauty-services marketplace. It is a two-sided, one-time-purchase business that leaks after the first booking. Urban Company took about 10 years to positive adjusted EBITDA. Blow LTD was liquidated (S-INT-12, S-INT-25).
   - Owned Delhi NCR centre: rent is 3.5x, it is the most crowded premium market, and the brand has no recognition there.
   - Becoming a franchisee of a national chain: the founder brand, PNA's only asset, would be lost.
   - Government-scheme B2G training: a different operating model, at about ₹8k per candidate (PMKVY average award).
   - Owned tricity satellites now: the satellite towns are contiguous (20–40 min), so they would cannibalise the flagship.
6. **Hypotheses:**
   - **H1** is supported at the ceiling (tricity caps one centre at about ₹0.6–1.7 crore) but is **not the binding constraint for 18–24 months**. Capacity, visibility and founder time are.
   - **H2** is plausible but unproven; test it cheaply.
   - **H3** is strongly supported as a constraint. As an asset it is unverified outside the referral circle.
   - **H4** is directionally supported on price realisation for hands-on bridal work. The completion advantage is unproven, and live-cohort design may matter more than "offline".
   - **H5** is strongly supported. A light alumni booking desk captures most of the benefit at near-zero capex.
7. **Directional year-3 shape of the primary model (A-BM-17) [EST]:**
   - Academy revenue of about **₹0.55 / 1.3 / 2.4 crore** (low/base/high), against about ₹0.3 crore today.
   - The base implies about 105 professional-programme students (about 8% of the tricity base market including out-of-town students) plus about 280 masterclass seats.
   - Stage 1 fits inside the ₹10–50 lakh envelope. The ₹10 lakh case funds only the minimum version (section 6.5).
8. **What Economics must test (section 7), in order:**
   - Flagship cohort unit economics and breakeven fill.
   - Founder-hours trajectory against conversion, the "step-back stress test".
   - Destination student versus owned/partner/franchise centre in Ludhiana.
   - Pop-up conversion economics.
   - Investment phasing at ₹10/30/50 lakh, with GST and EMI timing.

### A. Chandigarh flagship (win the catchment)

**Design [REC-candidate].** Convert on-demand classes into a structured academy:

- **Product ladder:**
  - P4 discovery workshop: 2–4 h, ₹999–1,999
  - P3 self-makeup: 1–3 days, ₹3–10k
  - P1 professional bridal programme: 10–12 weeks, list ₹90k–1.25 lakh, realised ₹65k / 80k / 1.0 lakh (A-BM-02)
  - P2 advanced masterclasses: ₹5–35k
  - P6 optional kit and portfolio day
- **Batches:** fixed cohorts capped at 12 (A-BM-01), run as morning, afternoon and weekend slots.
- **Team:** one lead trainer plus one trainer by month 12; an admissions counsellor on a WhatsApp CRM.
- **Funnel and proof:** published fees and EMI; published alumni outcomes.
- **Space:** in the studio if it holds 8–12 stations; otherwise 1,200–1,500 sq ft nearby (founder question).
- **Positioning:** a boutique studio-academy in Sector 8 that teaches on live brides, set against the Sector 34A institute cluster (S-LC §7 #4).

### D. Hybrid (online theory/discovery + offline practice, assessment, certification)

**Design [REC-candidate].** The "PNA Method" online pre-work is 50–70 hours: skin and colour theory, tools and hygiene, bridal look library, client handling, pricing, Instagram portfolio. It is delivered as recorded modules with weekly live Q&A. Offline practice follows on models and live brides, then assessment and a portfolio shoot (A-BM-03). Two variants:

- **D-local:** pre-work shortens and de-risks the 10–12-week offline programme for tricity students.
- **D-destination:** 3–4 weeks of online pre-work, then a 5–6-week Chandigarh intensive. This cuts an out-of-town student's stay from about 12 weeks to about 6 and her living cost from **₹33–54k to ₹17–28k** (A-BM-12; S-CHD-20/21).

**Verdict: reject (H5 strongly supported).**

**E-lite: the alumni booking desk [REC, inside the primary model].** The studio already receives bridal and family enquiries. Overflow, budget-tier and guest-makeup jobs go to a vetted roster of "PNA-certified" alumni (quality-checked portfolio, trial look) for a referral fee of 10–15% or a flat ₹1–2.5k (A-BM-09). Regional enquiries (e.g. Ludhiana) go to regional alumni. Why it is worth doing:

- **Outcome proof:** it produces the "% of alumni with a paid booking within 90 days" figure, the single most persuasive trust asset (S-CUS §4.1, §9).
- **Real assistant work in season:** it gives alumni paid work during the peak.
- **No platform build:** it needs a WhatsApp group and a booking sheet.

Revenue is small (about ₹1–3 lakh a year by year 3). The value lies in conversion and retention. Separately, place alumni on existing platforms (Urban Company, salon chains), which absorb trained professionals (S-INT §5.3).

### 7.2 Scenarios and stress tests that matter

1. **Low / Base / High** consistent with CA-01..05 and A-BM-02/17. The base must not imply >10% of CA-05 base tricity students by Y3 without the out-of-town engine. Flag if it does.
2. **Founder step-back stress (H3):** team-led conversion = founder-led ÷ 1.2–1.5 (A-CUS-30). Realised fee −10% when founder teaching drops below 30%. Does G1/G2 still clear?
3. **Price stress:** P1 realised at CA-02 (₹60k) instead of A-BM-02 (₹80k).
4. **Market stress:** CA-05 low case (593 students, ₹1.8 cr). What share does the base plan then require?
5. **Out-of-town share** 10 / 25 / 40% of P1, with all-in-cost elasticity.
6. **Seasonality:** enrolment and trainer availability dip Nov–Feb (A-CHD-12; A-CUS-29); cash trough.
7. **Envelope:** ₹10 lakh minimum plan vs ₹30 lakh vs ₹50 lakh. Which gates are reachable in each?
8. **Space:** studio-hosted (no new rent) vs leased 1,200–1,500 sq ft at ₹47 / 80 / 130 per sq ft (S-CHD-22).
