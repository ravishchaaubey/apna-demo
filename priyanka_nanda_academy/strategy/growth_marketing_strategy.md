## 0. Summary

1. **Start point (to be verified):** PNA did not appear in our tool-limited, US-based searches; Instagram, Maps and Justdial were blocked. To be verified manually with the founder before the founder meeting (CA-07; S-LC §2, §5).    Competitors win with keyword-specific course pages, fee pages, WhatsApp, demos and EMI (S-LC §7). **If the manual check confirms the gap, the first ₹ goes into visibility, proof and response discipline, not ads.**
2. **The engine is a proof flywheel [REC]:**
   - Student work and alumni outcomes become content.
   - Content (organic plus a small paid push) brings WhatsApp leads.
   - A counsellor-run funnel converts them, closed at a visit or a paid taster.
   - Enrolled students produce portfolios, reviews and referrals, which become more proof.

   Each turn makes the next lead cheaper. It must work without the founder in the inbox (CA-04).
3. **Seven workstreams** are covered in §2–8: social, content, website, SEO/search (incl. GBP and AI-search), paid acquisition (with test design and stopping rules), CRM/automation, and referral/partnerships.
4. **Do not scale spend until tracking, follow-up discipline, capacity and unit economics are validated.** Five **spend gates** (§9) must be green for **4 consecutive weeks** before paid media rises above the test level of ₹40–60k/month:
   - **SG1 Tracking**
   - **SG2 Follow-up discipline**
   - **SG3 Capacity**
   - **SG4 Unit economics**
   - **SG5 Product proof**

   Two stage gates also cap spend (recommended_strategy §5, §7.1): ≤₹15k/month until **G0 Instrumented baseline** (target M3, Jan 2027), and ≤₹60k/month until **G1 Flagship proven** (planning case M9 = Jul 2027; M6 = upside). Until then the job is learning, not volume. **SG4 note:** the Year-1 plan runs at about 49% fully loaded acquisition on the step-1 fee. That is amber (founder review), not a pass: an accepted Year-1 inefficiency while proof is built. The CAC range for the same target is ~9× wide (`admissions_funnel.md` §10.2).

**Production system:**
- One monthly 2-day batch shoot (≈20 Reels' worth).
- A **portfolio-day shoot for every batch** with standard lighting and before/after pairs.
- Consent forms at enrolment for image use (DPDP-compliant consent; see tech file §10).
- A shared content calendar.

**Proof system (student outcomes) [REC]:**
1. Tag the student's handle on every student-work post.
2. Store an "alumni wall" of verified handles.
3. Run the quarterly alumni survey (V4) and turn the results into an outcomes card (n, dates, medians).
4. Never post best-case earnings as typical (S-CUS §4.2).

**Founder vs brand on social:** follow the trust-transfer phases in `positioning_and_segments.md` §4.3.
- Founder appears in ~50% of academy content in months 0–6.
- Trainers and students appear in ≥40% by month 12.
- By month 12 the academy account generates ≥60% of tracked organic leads [HYP target].

### 4.3 Conversion mechanics [REC]
- **Click-to-WhatsApp** with a **pre-filled, source-coded message** per page, e.g., "Hi PNA, I'd like fees & next batch for the Pro Bridal course [W-PRO]". The code maps to the CRM source (tech file §5.3).
- **Sticky mobile bar:** WhatsApp | Call (tracked number) | Book visit.
- **Short fallback form** (name, WhatsApp number, course, city, start month, consent tick) that triggers an instant WhatsApp template.
- **Booking:** visit/taster slots via a booking tool or WhatsApp Flow. Taster and self-makeup purchases go through payment pages (tech file §3).
- **Performance standards:** mobile-first; aim for Core Web Vitals "good" (LCP <2.5 s); compressed images; no autoplay heavy video above the fold.

### 4.4 Analytics on site [REC]
- GA4 + GTM events: `whatsapp_click` (course, page), `call_click`, `generate_lead` (form), `book_visit`, `taster_purchase`, `fee_page_view`, `brochure_download`, `scroll_75`.
- Microsoft Clarity for heatmaps/recordings (free).
- UTMs on every bio, GBP and ad link. Full attribution design is in the tech file §8.

### 6.6 Stopping and scaling rules (all paid cells) [REC]

| Rule | Threshold |
|---|---|
| **Minimum learning spend** before judging a cell | ≥3× target cost per QL **and** ≥7 days (e.g., CTWA ≥₹2,250; Search ≥₹3,300) |
| **Kill** (KT-4) | Cost per QL >2× target after minimum spend (CTWA >₹1,500; Search >₹2,200), **or** zero QLs after ₹5,000. CAC payback >6 months for 2 quarters on any channel: cut the channel and reallocate to referral and Search |
| **Creative fatigue** | Frequency >3.0 in 7 days with CTR down >30% from the first week → refresh creative |
| **Hold** (do not increase) | Any spend gate (SG1–SG5) amber/red; media at the stage-gate cap (≤₹15k pre-G0; ≤₹60k pre-G1); counsellor load >30 conversations/day; next batch >80% full with no further batch scheduled |
| **Graduate to scale** | ≥30 QLs in the cell at ≥20% below target cost per QL **and** spend gates green (plus stage gate G1 for media above ₹60k/month) |
| **Scale step** | +20–30% budget per week per campaign; never double overnight. Re-check cost per QL after each step |
| **Roll back** | If cost per QL rises >25% above the pre-step level for 2 consecutive weeks, return to the previous budget |
| **Enrolment confirmation** | At 8–10 weeks, compare enrolments per route against A-GRO-18..22 base. A route below its Low rate is cut to test level and diagnosed (lead quality vs counselling) |
| **Learning phase (Meta)** | Meta guidance is that ad sets need roughly 50 optimisation events per week to stabilise [CLAIM, Meta Business Help, not verified this pass]. At PNA volumes, consolidate ad sets rather than fragment them |

## 12. Key risks and mitigations

| Risk | Signal | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Founder becomes the inbox and the bottleneck (H3, CA-04) | Founder-touch share >50% after month 6; SLA misses in wedding season | Counsellor owns the inbox; founder escalation slots only; trust-transfer phases |
| Proof does not exist or is weak | Outcomes survey response <30%; few reviews | Incentivised survey (free masterclass seat); portfolio-day system; publish a baseline honestly |
| Benchmarks are optimistic (vendor-sourced CPLs) | Cost per QL >2× target in T1–T2 | Stopping rules; shift to organic/referral; taster-led funnel |
| Capacity squeeze during the S1 peak | Batches full; counsellor >30 conversations/day | SG3; add a Flex batch; second counsellor (0.5 FTE after G1) |
| Price step backfires | QL rate or counselled→applied drops >20% after the change | Step gated on proof; T5; keep Foundation as the entry |
| Out-of-town safety incident | — | Vetted partners, written protocol, local guardian forms, insurance advice from counsel |
| Misleading-claim exposure | Ads with income/placement promises | Message-house "avoid" list; counsel review of outcome claims (S-CUS §4.2) |
| Platform/policy changes (WhatsApp pricing, Meta targeting) | Rate changes (India marketing rate up 1 Jan 2026; INR billing migration by 31 Dec 2026, S-GRO-01) | Budget buffer; owned channels (SEO, GBP, community) |
