## 0. Summary

1. **Start point:** PNA has a website and nothing else we could verify:
   - no Google Business Profile, Instagram, reviews or published proof was found
   - it is invisible for every non-branded search tested (CA-07; S-LC §2, §5)

   Competitors win with keyword-specific course pages, fee pages, WhatsApp, demos and EMI (S-LC §7). **The first ₹ goes into visibility, proof and response discipline, not ads.**
2. **The engine is a proof flywheel [REC]:**
   - Student work and alumni outcomes become content.
   - Content (organic plus a small paid push) brings WhatsApp leads.
   - A counsellor-run funnel converts them, closed at a visit or a paid taster.
   - Enrolled students produce portfolios, reviews and referrals, which become more proof.

   Each turn makes the next lead cheaper. It must work without the founder in the inbox (CA-04).
3. **Seven workstreams** are covered in §2–8: social, content, website, SEO/search (incl. GBP and AI-search), paid acquisition (with test design and stopping rules), CRM/automation, and referral/partnerships.
4. **Do not scale spend until tracking, follow-up discipline, capacity and unit economics are validated.** Five gates (§9) must be green for **4 consecutive weeks** before paid media rises above the test level of ₹40–60k/month:
   - **G1 Tracking**
   - **G2 Follow-up discipline**
   - **G3 Capacity**
   - **G4 Unit economics**
   - **G5 Product proof**

   Until then the job is learning, not volume. The CAC range for the same target is ~9× wide (`admissions_funnel.md` §10.2).
5. **12-month growth budget inside CA-03 (₹10–50L) [EST]:**

   | Scenario | 12-month total | Paid media in months 7–12 | Target |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | Conservative | **≈₹10L** | ₹40k/month | Protect learning; about 6 career enrolments/month by month 12 |
   | Base | **≈₹30L** | ₹1.0L/month | About 10/month |
   | Aggressive | **≈₹50L** | ₹2.0L/month | About 15/month + regional cohorts; **only if gates hold** |

   Team and production are the biggest non-media lines (§10).
6. **Timing matters.** It is 16 Oct 2026, so the wedding season (Nov–Feb; CAIT peak 1 Nov–14 Dec, S-CUS-13) starts now and fills the founder's calendar:
   - Use Nov–Feb to **fix foundations, capture real-bride content, sell S5 self-makeup and run S3 masterclasses in the Jan–Feb lean months**.
   - Run the **paid tests Dec–Feb**.
   - **Scale for the S1 peak, Mar–Jul 2027** (A-GRO-40).

**Production system:**
- One monthly 2-day batch shoot (≈20 Reels' worth).
- A **portfolio-day shoot for every batch** with standard lighting and before/after pairs.
- Consent forms at enrolment for image use (DPDP-compliant consent; see tech file §10).
- A shared content calendar.

**Proof system (student outcomes) [REC]:**
1. Tag the student's handle on every student-work post.
2. Store an "alumni wall" of verified handles.
3. Run the quarterly alumni survey (V4) and turn the results into an outcomes card (n, dates, medians).
4. Never post best-case earnings as typical (S-CUS §4.2).

**Founder vs brand on social:** follow the trust-transfer phases in `positioning_and_segments.md` §4.3.
- Founder appears in ~50% of academy content in months 0–6.
- Trainers and students appear in ≥40% by month 12.
- By month 12 the academy account generates ≥60% of tracked organic leads [HYP target].

### 4.3 Conversion mechanics [REC]
- **Click-to-WhatsApp** with a **pre-filled, source-coded message** per page, e.g., "Hi PNA, I'd like fees & next batch for the Pro Bridal course [W-PRO]". The code maps to the CRM source (tech file §5.3).
- **Sticky mobile bar:** WhatsApp | Call (tracked number) | Book visit.
- **Short fallback form** (name, WhatsApp number, course, city, start month, consent tick) that triggers an instant WhatsApp template.
- **Booking:** visit/taster slots via a booking tool or WhatsApp Flow. Taster and self-makeup purchases go through payment pages (tech file §3).
- **Performance standards:** mobile-first; aim for Core Web Vitals "good" (LCP <2.5 s); compressed images; no autoplay heavy video above the fold.

### 4.4 Analytics on site [REC]
- GA4 + GTM events: `whatsapp_click` (course, page), `call_click`, `generate_lead` (form), `book_visit`, `taster_purchase`, `fee_page_view`, `brochure_download`, `scroll_75`.
- Microsoft Clarity for heatmaps/recordings (free).
- UTMs on every bio, GBP and ad link. Full attribution design is in the tech file §8.

### 5.3 AI-search discoverability (Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity) [REC]

1. **Entity consistency:** the same name, address, phone, founder name, course names and fees on the website, GBP, Instagram bio, YouTube and directories.
2. **Structured data:**
   - `EducationalOrganization` + `LocalBusiness` (address, geo, hours)
   - `Course` with `offers` (price, currency) and `hasCourseInstance` (dates, mode)
   - `Person` (founder, educators)
   - `FAQPage`
   - `Review` only where it complies with Google's guidelines
3. **Answer-shaped pages:** plain-language Q&A blocks ("How much does a bridal makeup course cost in Chandigarh?", "How many practice hours?", "Is the certificate recognised?") with specific numbers and dates.
4. **Citable evidence:** the Alumni Outcomes Report with n and dates, and the published fee ladder. Specific, dated facts are what answer engines quote [HYP].
5. **Third-party corroboration:** local press, directory listings, alumni Instagram tags linking back.
6. **Monthly prompt audit:** run 10 fixed prompts (e.g., "best bridal makeup academy in Chandigarh with fees", "makeup course for outstation students in Chandigarh") in ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google. Log whether PNA appears, where, and which sources are cited.

## 6. Workstream 5: Paid acquisition

### 6.1 Principles [REC]
1. **Paid amplifies proof; it does not create it.** No scale until the gates in §9 are passed.
2. **Optimise to quality signals, not clicks.** Feed QL and Enrolled back to Meta (Conversions API for business messaging / CTWA events) and to Google (offline conversion import / enhanced conversions for leads). See tech file §8.

### 6.5 Test design [REC]

All tests use **matched budgets**, run at the same time, with **one variable** changed. The primary metric is **cost per QL** at 2–4 weeks (leading indicator), confirmed on **cost per counselled** and **enrolments at 8–10 weeks** (lagging). Sample targets follow S-CUS §10 (V6: ≥150 leads per arm where feasible).

| # | Hypothesis | Arms | Budget / duration | Sample target | Primary metric | Decision rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **T1** (H6) | CTWA + WhatsApp Flow yields cheaper QLs than an instant lead form followed up on WhatsApp | A: CTWA + Flow; B: Meta instant form → auto WhatsApp template | ₹25k per arm, 4–5 weeks | ≥150 leads per arm (or ≥40 QLs per arm) | Cost per QL; QL→counselled | Winner = ≥20% lower cost per QL with QL→counselled no worse than −10 pts. Otherwise keep CTWA (lower ops friction) |
| **T2** | Proof creative beats founder creative on QL rate | R1 (student proof) vs R2/R7 (founder) vs R3 (fee transparency) vs R12 (Day 1) | ₹4–6k per creative within M1 | ≥3,000 impressions and ≥₹2k per creative before judging | Cost per conversation → cost per QL | Pause a creative at <50% of the ad-set median CTR after 3,000 impressions, or cost per QL >2× target (₹1,500) after ₹3k |
| **T3** | A paid ₹999 taster qualifies better than a free online info session for S2 / undecided S1 | A: ₹999 taster (credited); B: free online session | ₹15k per arm, 8 weeks (2 sessions each) | ≥30 ticket buyers vs ≥60 registrants | Counselled per ₹1,000 spent; enrolments ≤60 days | Keep the arm with lower cost per enrolment at 60 days; if ties, prefer the taster (it self-liquidates) |
| **T4** | For S1, taster-first converts as well as counselling-first and needs less founder/counsellor time | A: CTWA → counselling → visit; B: CTWA → taster offer | Within M1, 8 weeks | ≥100 leads per arm | Enrolment per lead at 60 days; counsellor minutes per enrolment | Adopt B for S1 if enrolment per lead ≥0.8× A and counsellor time ≤0.6× A |
| **T5** | Showing the fee on the landing page raises QL rate (fewer non-fits) without crushing volume | Pro page fee shown vs gated | Search traffic split, 6 weeks | ≥80 leads per arm | Cost per QL | Keep "fee shown" unless cost per QL is >20% worse |
| **T6** (H2) | Regional geos deliver qualified out-of-town leads at an acceptable cost | M2 cities vs M1 tricity | ₹15k regional, 6 weeks | ≥100 regional leads | Cost per S7 QL; parents' session attendance | Continue to Phase 3 if cost per S7 QL ≤1.3× tricity |
| **T7** | Search course-intent outperforms Meta on cost per counselled | G2 vs M1 (observational) | Running budgets | — | Cost per counselled | Re-allocate monthly toward the cheaper route (max ±30%) |
| **T8** | Referral reward level | ₹2,000 vs ₹3,000 (or masterclass credit) by alumni cohort | 1 quarter | ≥40 alumni per arm | Referral leads per alumnus | Pick the cheaper reward per referral enrolment |

### 6.6 Stopping and scaling rules (all paid cells) [REC]

| Rule | Threshold |
|---|---|
| **Minimum learning spend** before judging a cell | ≥3× target cost per QL **and** ≥7 days (e.g., CTWA ≥₹2,250; Search ≥₹3,300) |
| **Kill** | Cost per QL >2× target after minimum spend (CTWA >₹1,500; Search >₹2,200), **or** zero QLs after ₹5,000 |
| **Creative fatigue** | Frequency >3.0 in 7 days with CTR down >30% from the first week → refresh creative |
| **Hold** (do not increase) | Any gate in §9 amber/red; counsellor load >30 conversations/day; next batch >80% full with no further batch scheduled |
| **Graduate to scale** | ≥30 QLs in the cell at ≥20% below target cost per QL **and** gates green |
| **Scale step** | +20–30% budget per week per campaign; never double overnight. Re-check cost per QL after each step |
| **Roll back** | If cost per QL rises >25% above the pre-step level for 2 consecutive weeks, return to the previous budget |
| **Enrolment confirmation** | At 8–10 weeks, compare enrolments per route against A-GRO-18..22 base. A route below its Low rate is cut to test level and diagnosed (lead quality vs counselling) |
| **Learning phase (Meta)** | Meta guidance is that ad sets need roughly 50 optimisation events per week to stabilise [CLAIM, Meta Business Help, not verified this pass]. At PNA volumes, consolidate ad sets rather than fragment them |

## 9. Scale-up gates: **do not scale spend until all five are green**

Paid media stays at **test level (₹40–60k/month)** until every gate is green for **4 consecutive weekly reviews**. Gate thresholds are **[HYP]**; the Economics agent should re-set G4 from `funnel_economics.xlsx`.

| Gate | What it validates | Metric and threshold | Data source | If not met |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **G1 Tracking** | We can see the funnel | ≥95% of leads carry a source tag; CTWA referral/click ID captured; GA4 + Meta (CAPI) + Google offline conversions firing for Lead, QL, Enrolled; **CRM enrolments reconcile with accounts within ±5%**; dashboard published 4 weeks running | CRM, GA4, Events Manager, accounts | Fix before any test result is trusted |
| **G2 Follow-up discipline** | Leads are worked fast and fully | **Median first response <5 min, P90 <30 min** (working hours); ≥90% of leads human-contacted within 1 working hour; ≥90% of open QLs with ≥5 touches in 14 days; lost reason recorded for ≥90% of closed leads | CRM timestamps | Coach and re-staff; A-GRO-24 penalty applies to forecasts |
| **G3 Capacity** | Enrolments can be delivered well | Next two batches scheduled with seats ≥ forecast enrolments; trainer ratio ≤1:12 (≤1:6 practicals); live-model supply booked; counsellor load ≤30 conversations/day; **founder hours within the agreed budget** (CA-04) | Batch calendar, CRM | Add a batch/trainer first, or cap spend at fill rate |
| **G4 Unit economics** | Growth is profitable | Trailing 8 weeks with ≥12 paid career enrolments: **media CAC per paid career enrolment ≤₹9,000** (≈13% of a weighted ₹70k fee [EST]); **fully loaded acquisition cost** (media + growth team + tools + rewards + EMI subvention) **≤25% of career revenue booked**; each route's lead→enrol ≥ its Low rate (A-GRO-18..22) | CRM + accounts | Cut to test level; diagnose by route (lead quality vs counselling vs offer) |
| **G5 Product proof** | We are amplifying a good product | Completion ≥85% (A-GRO-31); NPS ≥50; ≥30 Google reviews at ≥4.5 average; first Alumni Outcomes Report published (n ≥30); refunds/drops <10% | Academic records, GBP, survey | Fix delivery before buying more demand |

**Scale protocol once green [REC]:**
- Raise budgets by 20–30% per week on graduated cells (§6.6).
- Re-check G2–G4 weekly.
- Any gate turning amber freezes increases. Any gate turning red rolls spend back to the last green level.

## 12. Key risks and mitigations

| Risk | Signal | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Founder becomes the inbox and the bottleneck (H3, CA-04) | Founder-touch share >50% after month 6; SLA misses in wedding season | Counsellor owns the inbox; founder escalation slots only; trust-transfer phases |
