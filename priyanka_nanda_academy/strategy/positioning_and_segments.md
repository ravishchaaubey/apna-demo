## 1. Summary

1. **Segment priorities (adopted from customer research, refined) [REC]:**
   - **Core (revenue):** S1 aspiring professional MUAs aged 18–25 in the tricity.
   - **Flywheel (profit and brand):** S3 working artists and salon staff upskilling.
   - **Growth bets:** S7 out-of-town students from Punjab, HP, Haryana and J&K (an overlay on S1/S3), and S2 career-switchers and homemakers aged 25–40.
   - **Top-of-funnel:** S5 self-makeup learners, as a seasonal top-of-funnel and cash product, and S6 creators as amplifiers.
   - **Defer:** S4 government-scheme skilling.
   - **Treat as a decision unit, not a segment:** parents and spouses. They get their own message track.
2. **Positioning [REC]:** *the small-batch bridal makeup academy inside a working bridal studio*. Students learn the **PNA Method** on real faces, leave with a portfolio, and can check published fees and published alumni outcomes before paying. This deliberately contrasts with the Sector 34A institute cluster (Orane, Lakmé, VLCC, Zuri, Cassia), which sells breadth, certificates and "100% placement" with hidden fees (S-LC-08..23; S-NC §5.2).
3. **Founder → institution trust transfer [REC]:** the founder will step back from daily teaching within 24–36 months (CA-04). Trust therefore has to move to three assets the business owns:
   - a codified **PNA Method**
   - **PNA Certified Educators** with their own visible portfolios
   - **published alumni outcomes**

   The founder keeps bounded, high-leverage touchpoints: the welcome class, one signature masterclass per batch, portfolio-day assessment, a monthly live session and graduation. Her hours are stated per course.
4. **Course ladder [HYP prices]:**

   | Step | Product | Price |
   |---|---|---|
   | Entry | Paid Career Taster | ₹999 |
   | Entry | Self-Makeup workshop | ₹4,999 |
   | Foundation | Foundation (4 weeks) | ₹39,900 |
   | Flagship | **L3 Pro: Professional Bridal Makeup Artist Programme** (~280–300 h; Weekday 12 weeks / Flex ~20 weeks / Destination 4 weeks online pre-work + 5–6-week intensive) | Current fee → ₹75,000 list (step 1) → ₹90,000 list (step 2); ₹80k realised planning fee; pro kit optional |
   | Specialisation | Masterclasses | ₹4k–20k, average ₹10k |
   | Membership | PNA Pro Circle | ₹9,999/yr |

   Credits carry upward: the taster fee is credited to the Foundation or Pro course, and the Foundation fee is credited to Pro. The flagship price sits above the local 3–6 month diploma band (₹35k–75k, S-LC §6) and below the chain flagships (₹1.3–1.5L, S-NC-02). The proof assets must justify that position, and **the price should not rise above PNA's current fee (CA-02, unknown) until proof gate 1 is met** (first outcomes report n≥30, ≥30 Google reviews at ≥4.5, portfolio gallery, published practice hours; recommended_strategy §7.2).
5. **The main gap is that the proof has not been found yet.** We found no Google rating, Instagram handle, testimonial or outcome data for PNA (CA-07, S-LC §2). Positioning claims below that depend on proof are marked "build" and must not be advertised until they exist.

## 4. Founder brand vs institution brand

### 4.1 Diagnosis
- **H3 holds directionally.** S3 buys "the founder" (S-CUS §3.2). Founder-led counselling is assumed to convert 1.2–1.5× better than team-led counselling (A-CUS-30).
- The international cases show both the asset and the risk:
  - Mao Geping: the spouse ran the institute day to day, and a student complained the founder appeared only for graduation photos (S-INT-01/05).
  - Glauca Rossi: the founder-named academy stopped offering courses (S-INT-41).
- The founder will step back from daily teaching (CA-04). If the brand promise stays "learn from Priyanka", it becomes untrue at scale. That is a conduct risk as well as a growth cap.

### 4.2 Brand architecture [REC]

| Layer | Name (working) | What it carries | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| Masterbrand | **Priyanka Nanda Makeup Academy**, short form **"PNA"** | Heritage and the founder's name. **Keep the name, no rename now.** No equity yet justifies a rebrand, and the current domain ranks only for the branded query (S-LC-51) | Founder |
| Curriculum brand | **The PNA Method** | 8–12 named signature techniques (e.g., "PNA humid-weather bridal base", "3-tone Punjabi bridal eye" [HYP names]); written standards, assessment rubrics | Founder (curriculum owner, CA-04) |
| People brand | **PNA Certified Educator** | Trainers assessed and certified by the founder. Each has a public profile and portfolio | Academic head |
| Outcomes brand | **PNA Alumni** + "Find a PNA Artist" directory | Verifiable graduates with Instagram handles, cities and booking proof | Alumni / marketing |
| Community / membership | **PNA Pro Circle** | Ongoing learning, brand discounts, seasonal assisting gigs | Marketing |
| Sister business | **Priyanka Nanda Studio** (bridal services) | The founder's bridal work. Supplies live practice and proof for the academy | Founder |

**Social accounts [REC]:**
- Keep (or create) **two Instagram accounts**: the founder's personal/bridal account, which is the halo, and an **academy account**, which is the institution.
- Use Instagram Collab posts so the founder's audience flows into the academy account.
- By month 12, the academy account should be the main acquisition asset (≥60% of tracked organic leads) [HYP target].

Handles are unknown today (CA-07). Founder to supply.

### 4.3 Trust-transfer roadmap [REC]

| Phase | Timing | Founder's visible role | Trainer visibility | Alumni proof | Measures |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Founder-fronted** | Months 0–6 | Teaches the core bridal modules; on ~50% of academy content; joins counselling only for escalations (≤5 founder calls/week) | Trainers co-teach and appear in "trainer spotlight" content (≥1 per month) | First outcomes survey (V4, S-CUS §10); portfolio-day content for every batch | Founder-touch flag in CRM; conversion founder-led vs team-led (A-GRO-25) |
| **2. Co-led** | Months 6–18 | **Bounded touchpoints per Pro batch:** Day-1 welcome masterclass, one signature masterclass, portfolio-day assessment and sign-off ("Assessed by Priyanka Nanda"), graduation. **Stated as "≥12 hours with Priyanka per Pro batch"** [HYP number] | Educators lead daily teaching; 2–3 certified educators with their own pages; ≥40% of Reels feature trainers or students | First public **Alumni Outcomes Report** (n, dates, medians); alumni directory live | G1 trainer-parity metrics (recommended_strategy §7.1): trainer-led cohort satisfaction within 0.3/5 of founder-led; team-led counselling conversion ≥75% of founder-led. **Founder share of contact hours ≤40% at M12, ≤30% at M18** (recommended_strategy §10 #2) |
| **3. Institution-led** | Months 18–36 | Brand face, curriculum owner, masterclass lead (CA-04); monthly "Ask Priyanka" live; annual PNA Method update | Educator team carries delivery; senior educator heads the academy | Two years of outcome data; alumni as guest trainers and regional ambassadors | Share of enrolments with no founder touch ≥70% while conversion holds; founder share of contact hours ≤15% at M24, ≤10% at M36 |

**Rules [REC]:**
1. **Never imply the founder teaches everything.** State founder hours per course on every course page. This is honest, and it protects the price of founder-led masterclasses (S3).
2. **Make trainers famous.** Publish their portfolios, their years of experience and their own student work. Content credits the student and the trainer by name.
3. **Make the method visible.** Name the techniques, show the before/after of each, and assess against a published rubric.
4. **Make outcomes the brand.** Over time "PNA" should mean "graduates who get booked", not "Priyanka's class".

**Ladder logic [REC]:**
- **Credits carry upward.** L1 → L2/L3 and L2 → L3. Every low-ticket purchase is a down-payment on the flagship, not a substitute for it. This addresses the cannibalisation risk (S-CUS §6c).
- **One flagship, three formats.** Weekday, Flex and Destination share one curriculum and one rubric, so trainers can deliver them without the founder.
- **Practice hours are published, not equated to NSQF.** PNA Pro (~280–300 h) sits above the chain flagships' stated hours (Lakmé 152–260 h, Orane 120–218 h; S-LC-19/21, S-LC-10) and below the NSQF L4 qualification (510 h, S-CUS-10). Publish the hours honestly. Do not claim equivalence.
- **Kit is optional and itemised.** This answers the "value for money" objection and avoids the bundled-purchase conduct risk seen internationally (S-INT-03/04).

**Pricing rules [REC]:**
1. **Publish the ladder** on the website and Google Business Profile. Show "from ₹X/month" EMI next to every fee.
2. **No fake strike-through discounts.** If there is an early-bird, it is date-based and real (e.g., ₹5,000 off if the token is paid ≥30 days before start [HYP]).
3. **Raise the flagship price in steps tied to proof.** Hold the current fee (CA-02) for the next two batches while the outcomes survey and portfolio system are built. Then follow the **two-step path** (recommended_strategy §7.2): **step 1 → ₹75,000 list** once the first Alumni Outcomes Report (n≥30), ≥30 Google reviews at ≥4.5, the portfolio gallery and published practice hours exist; **step 2 → ₹90,000 list (₹80k realised)** once a second outcomes report with "% paid booking ≤90 days" exists, 2 trainer-led cohorts are ≥75% full and step-1 conversion held within −20%. Roll back for one intake if QL rate or counselled→applied falls >20% (KT-5). Run a price test (the published fee on two landing-page variants) only after spend gates SG1–SG2 in `growth_marketing_strategy.md` §9 are met.
4. **Run a fee-transparency test.** Test "fee shown" against "fee on WhatsApp" on Pro landing pages. Measure QL rate and cost per QL (`growth_marketing_strategy.md` §6.5, test T5).
