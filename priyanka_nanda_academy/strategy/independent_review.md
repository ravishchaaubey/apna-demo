## 1. Verdict: **Approve with conditions**

1. The strategic direction is right and well argued: one Chandigarh flagship, proof before price, staged money, destination learning gated at G2, and explicit rejection of the marketplace, an owned NCR or Ludhiana centre, franchising and the ₹50L plan. The rejections are rejected for the right reasons and the evidence discipline (tags, assumption IDs, one source of numbers) is well above the norm. The arithmetic chain research → workbooks → strategy → execution reconciles on every headline figure I traced (about 30 figures; list in §4).
2. The financial framing is not yet investor-grade. The primary financial view (G1 at M6: ₹66.5L / +₹2.2L / peak ₹25.6L / 91 enrolments) rests on a gate decision on 9 April 2027 whose evidence cannot exist by that date on the plan's own numbers: the model's monthly enrolments give the 4 January cohort about 5 L3 students (42% fill) and the 1 March cohort about 7–8 (60%), against a G1 test of two consecutive cohorts ≥75% full (RT-02).
3. The downside the founder actually faces on the ₹30–35L path is never modelled. "Conservative" is adverse rates bundled with the ₹10L plan; there is no run of the Base team at adverse rates, and no run of a Tranche-2 commitment followed by a weak peak season. My estimates from the workbook cells put the first at about ₹18L by end-March (through the ₹15L Tranche-1 cap in February) and the second at ₹33–48L by October 2027 with a signed lease (RT-01). The "₹10L-plan stop-loss" is unavailable once T2 is spent.
4. Nobody owns execution. The 90-day plan has 13 workstreams and about ten hires or contractors, run by a ₹20k/month performance freelancer "extended" to chair it, with the founder as de facto programme manager in her bridal peak season (RT-03). Founder dependency in Year 1 is shifted (share of hours falls to 40%) rather than reduced (modelled teaching rises to 106–128 hours a month from May) (RT-04).
5. Conditions for approval are in §7. None requires rewriting the strategy; most require one more model run and honest restatement of three or four sentences in the executive summary.

## 5. Questions a sceptical investor will ask that the materials do not yet answer

1. **What does PNA actually make today?** Every PNA number is an assumption (CA-01/02, A-ECO-101/110/118). Until D1 lands, the deck is a plan for a hypothetical academy. (Acknowledged everywhere; still the first question.)
2. **What is the return on my ₹30–35L?** No payback month, IRR or multiple is stated. From `3yr_Scenarios` row 55 the founder's cash turns positive only in Year 3 and is roughly break-even at M36 after paying herself ₹75k/month, before tax. Is a three-year payback on a key-person business, with a lease, worth the risk versus the ₹10L boutique at +₹10.8L a year?
3. **What is the worst I can lose, and when do I know?** Not stated in rupees for the recommended path (RT-01). "Gates catch it by M9" is asserted, not demonstrated for the T1 cost base.
4. **Who runs this?** No GM until M13; the founder is the programme manager in her peak season (RT-03).
5. **Why will a Chandigarh family pay ₹90k to an academy with no public reviews, when Zuri publishes a ladder from ₹25k, Orane offers 0% EMI next door and Lakmé's brand sells at ₹1.3–1.5L?** No willingness-to-pay evidence; zero price elasticity in the model (RT-05).
6. **What happens when Orane, Lakmé or Zuri copy "published fees and outcomes" in a week?** Competitor response is explicitly out of the model (methodology §7.4); the "white space" is contestable at zero cost.
7. **Can you hire the trainer?** No pay evidence; a salaried senior bridal MUA in wedding season is an untested labour-market assumption (RT-11).
8. **If the Lead Trainer leaves in July, or the founder is out for a month, what happens to cohort 2 and the price step?** No cover plan.
9. **How much of Year 3 is the flagship and how much is gated hope?** ≈₹43L of the ₹165L Y3 revenue sits behind gates G2/G3 or an audience threshold PNA is nowhere near (RT-10).
10. **Why spend ₹1.0L/month on media when your own funnel model says ₹37.6k buys the same enrolments?** The overhead factor is a calibration (RT-06); the growth team costs 1.5x the media.
11. **What does the founder live on in Year 1?** After-salary EBITDA is −₹6.9L; the studio's revenue, and how much of it survives 128 teaching hours a month, is unknown (open question 9).
12. **What is the lease?** Lock-in, exit, fire NOC, 12 stations on a second floor: none addressed (RT-20).
13. **Is the digital invisibility real?** The finding comes from a tool that could not open Instagram or Maps (RT-09).
14. **Who owns the PNA Method once a trainer has learned it?** IP, non-solicit and alumni-poaching risk are not covered; the step-back makes the method the asset.

## 6. What is strong and should be kept

1. **The core recommendation and its reasoning.** Flagship first; proof before price; two-step pricing gated on an outcomes report and reviews; kit optional and itemised; no placement or income promises; the studio as a separate P&L; destination learning before any second site; partner-hosted before owned; franchise last.
